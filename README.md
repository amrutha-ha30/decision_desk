
# 💰 Decision Desk — Think Before You Borrow

### A FinTech Solution for Smarter Emergency Borrowing Decisions

## 📌 About the Project

**Decision Desk** is a FinTech-based prototype designed to help people make smarter and more responsible financial decisions before borrowing money.

In today's digital world, many loan applications make borrowing money quick and easy. However, people often take loans during emergencies without fully understanding the repayment burden, interest, or the impact on their future financial stability.

We chose the **FinTech domain** to address this problem by building a simple decision-support platform that encourages users to pause, evaluate their financial situation, and understand the consequences of borrowing before making a final decision.

Decision Desk introduces a **60-minute decision window** before a user proceeds with an emergency borrowing request. This waiting period is designed to encourage thoughtful financial decisions instead of impulsive borrowing.

## 🚨 Problem Statement

People often face unexpected financial situations such as medical emergencies, urgent repairs, educational expenses, or travel emergencies.

During such situations:

- People may borrow money without understanding their repayment capacity.
- Instant loan applications can encourage quick borrowing decisions.
- Borrowers may overlook existing loans, expenses, and upcoming financial commitments.
- People may struggle to understand whether borrowing an additional amount is financially manageable.
- Impulsive borrowing may create additional financial stress in the future.

There is a need for a platform that helps users think, evaluate, and make informed borrowing decisions before taking financial commitments.

## 💡 Our Solution — Decision Desk

Decision Desk acts as a financial decision-support layer that helps users evaluate an emergency borrowing request before proceeding.

Instead of encouraging immediate borrowing, our prototype introduces a structured decision process that gives users time to reconsider their financial needs.

Our solution focuses on:

- Encouraging responsible borrowing.
- Providing a waiting period before a borrowing decision.
- Helping users understand the amount they intend to borrow.
- Promoting financial awareness.
- Supporting informed decision-making during emergencies.

## ⚙️ How Decision Desk Works

### 1. Emergency Borrowing Request

The user enters the amount they need and selects the reason for borrowing, such as:

- Medical Emergency
- Education
- Travel
- Unexpected Repairs
- Other Emergency Expenses

The platform begins the decision process based on the user's request.

### 2. 60-Minute Decision Window

Once the user initiates an emergency borrowing request, Decision Desk introduces a **60-minute waiting period** before the user proceeds to the next step of the borrowing process.

During this period, the user is encouraged to reconsider the necessity of the loan and evaluate the financial commitment.

The purpose of this waiting period is to reduce rushed financial decisions and encourage thoughtful borrowing.

### 3. Financial Decision Awareness

The prototype is designed around the idea that borrowers should consider the amount they need, the purpose of the loan, and their ability to manage future repayments.

Decision Desk focuses on making the borrowing decision more conscious and informed rather than encouraging users to borrow immediately.

### 4. Borrowing Decision

After the 60-minute decision window, the user can continue to the next stage of the prototype's borrowing journey.

The prototype demonstrates an emergency loan decision and approval flow to showcase how a pause-based financial decision system could work.

The waiting period is a decision-support feature, not a guarantee of loan approval or an actual financial transaction.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Emergency Loan Request:** Allows users to enter an emergency borrowing amount and purpose.
- **60-Minute Decision Window:** Introduces a pause before proceeding with the borrowing journey.
- **Responsible Borrowing Approach:** Encourages users to think about their financial commitments before borrowing.
- **Simple User Interface:** Designed to make the borrowing decision process understandable and accessible.
- **Prototype Loan Approval Flow:** Demonstrates the journey from an emergency request to the next stage of the borrowing process.
- **Financial Decision Support:** Focuses on helping users make thoughtful decisions rather than encouraging instant borrowing.

## 🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is not to prevent people from accessing emergency funds.

Instead, we want to make borrowing more thoughtful, transparent, and responsible.

Decision Desk aims to bridge the gap between **instant access to borrowing and informed financial decisions** by giving users an opportunity to pause before committing to a financial obligation.

## 🛠️ Prototype

We developed a simple interactive prototype to demonstrate the concept of Decision Desk.

The prototype showcases the emergency borrowing journey, the waiting-period concept, and the next step of the loan decision process.

This prototype represents our initial concept and can be further developed with financial data integration, repayment analysis, secure user consent, and real-world lending integrations in the future.
